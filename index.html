What Melzo does

Everything you expect from a music app, without the ads the app adds on top.

No ads added

Melzo adds no ads of its own. Nothing is sold, nothing is tracked, and no listening data is sent to the developer.

Your own account

You sign in on Spotify's own login page, so the app never sees your password. A free Spotify account is enough.

Offline downloads

Keep playlists on your phone for the metro, the flight or the bad network. Files stay encrypted on the device.

Synced lyrics

Word-by-word or line-by-line lyrics with translation, plus song meanings, so you can actually follow the track.

Community extensions

Small sandboxed add-ons for extra audio sources, lyrics and downloads. Every permission is shown before you install one.

69 languages

Including Indian languages. Pick the app's language separately from the language of the music you play.

Real player controls

10-band equalizer, crossfade, playback speed, sleep timer, drag-to-reorder queue and Smart Shuffle.

Podcasts & audiobooks

Their own player with speed control, ±15-second skips and mark-as-played, so episodes do not clutter your queue.

Everywhere you listen

Spotify Connect, Bluetooth, lock screen and notification controls, home-screen widget, Android Auto and Android TV.

Melzo — Spotify Pro extension

Adds YouTube Music as an audio source: it searches in the song's own language, picks the official recording, and lets Melzo download it for offline play. Install it once from the extension store inside the app.

How to install the extension

Install in three steps

No store account, no sign-up, no waiting.

  1. Download the APK from this page or from the GitHub release. It is the universal build, so it works on 32-bit and 64-bit phones alike.
  2. Open the file and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. That is a one-time switch per app.
  3. Sign in with Spotify, choose your sound quality, and you are in. Updates arrive inside the app.

Every release ships a matching .sha256 file if you want to check the download.

Questions people ask

Is Melzo free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under the GNU GPL v3. There is no paid tier, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

Do I need Spotify Premium?

No. A free Spotify account is enough to sign in and play.

Is my account safe?

You type your password on Spotify's own login page, never into Melzo. Playback runs through Spotify's own web player, so your plays are reported to Spotify exactly as they would be in a browser — they count for the artists and in your listening history.

Which Android versions work?

Android 10 or later. The same APK covers 32-bit and 64-bit phones; there are separate builds for Android TV and Wear OS in the release page.

How do I update?

Melzo checks for updates itself and installs them over the old version — your library, downloads and settings stay. You can also download the newest APK here and install it on top.

Is Melzo affiliated with Spotify?

No. Melzo is an independent app made by Prashant and is not affiliated with Spotify or any streaming service. You use your own account, at your own risk.
