Privacy Policy

Applies to the Melzo Android app, its extensions and this website. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Short version: Melzo has no account with the developer, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives your data. What leaves your phone goes to the streaming service you signed in to, and to the sites that provide lyrics or audio when you use an extension.

1. Who is responsible

Melzo is made by Prashant (@prakha194). There is no company behind it, no server of the developer's, and no database of users. Contact for any privacy question: open an issue on GitHub.

2. What the app never does

  • No account, sign-up, email or phone number is asked for.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs from the developer.
  • No listening history, search terms or device identifiers are sent to the developer.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties by the developer.

3. What the app sends, and to whom

Spotify

You sign in on Spotify's own login page, so Melzo never sees your password. Playback runs through Spotify's web player, which means your plays, searches and library requests reach Spotify exactly as they would in a browser, under Spotify's privacy policy. If you download songs for offline play, the files are kept encrypted on your phone.

Lyrics and song meanings

When you open lyrics or a meaning, the app queries lyrics sites, Genius and, if you ask for a translation, Google Translate. Those requests contain the song title and artist — nothing about you.

Extensions

Extensions are small add-ons published by other people. Each one declares the hosts it contacts, and Melzo shows you that list before you install it. An extension can only reach the hosts it declares. For example, the Spotify Pro extension contacts music.youtube.com, youtubei.googleapis.com and *.googlevideo.com to fetch audio. Read the list before you install, and only install extensions you trust.

Optional features

Live Radio, if you switch it on, asks the Radio Browser directory for station lists and then connects to the station's own server. Update checks and extension discovery contact GitHub (and GitLab or Codeberg if you add extensions from there).

4. What stays on your phone

  • Your login session and settings
  • Your downloads, encrypted with a key held on the device
  • Your local audio files
  • Your private listening recap — it is generated on the phone and never uploaded

Uninstalling the app removes this data. Clearing the app's storage does the same.

5. Permissions

Melzo asks only for what it uses: network access, storage for downloads and your own audio files, and notification access for playback controls. Any other permission is requested by the specific feature you switch on, and Android asks you at that moment.

6. Children

Melzo is not directed at children under 13. A Spotify account is required, and Spotify sets its own age rules.

7. This website

  • This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • The only script on the page asks GitHub for the newest release number so the download button stays current. That request goes to GitHub, not to the developer.
  • Like any web host, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Download links point to GitHub releases. GitHub may count downloads, as any file host does.

8. Your choices

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to export or delete on a server. On your phone you can clear the app's storage, remove downloads, or uninstall the app at any time. To stop data reaching Spotify, sign out inside the app.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is visible in the website's public history.

10. Not affiliated

Melzo is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Spotify, YouTube, Google or any streaming service or content provider, and those services handle your data under their own policies.
