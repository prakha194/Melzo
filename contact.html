Where your message should go

  • A bug — open an issue. It keeps the details in one place and helps the next person with the same problem.
  • An extension problem — the Spotify Pro extension has its own tracker: Melzo-Extension issues.
  • A security problem — do not post it publicly. Use the private report on the repository's Security tab, or email.
  • Anything else — Telegram or email, both fine.
Please do not send account passwords or screenshots of your Spotify login. Nobody needs them, and I will never ask for them.
