About Melzo

The app, how it works, and who makes it.

Melzo is a music app for Android. It is free, it is open source, and it adds no ads of its own. You sign in with your own Spotify account — a free one is enough — and the app plays your library, your playlists and your podcasts through Spotify's own player.

Why it exists

Because a music player should be fast, quiet and yours. Melzo keeps the parts people actually use — search, library, playlists, queue, lyrics, downloads — and drops the parts nobody asked for. No sign-up with the developer, no analytics, no tracking, no listening data leaving your phone for anyone but the streaming service you already use.

How it works

You sign in on Spotify's own login page, so the app never sees your password. Behind the scenes, Spotify's web player runs in a hidden view on your phone, using your phone's own DRM. Melzo draws its own native screens on top and controls it.

Because playback goes through Spotify's own player, your plays are reported to Spotify just like in a browser. They count for the artists, in your listening history and in your yearly recap.

What you get

  • Home, search, browse, new releases and a library with folders
  • Artist, album, playlist and profile pages
  • Shuffle, Smart Shuffle, repeat, crossfade, sleep timer and a drag-to-reorder queue
  • Podcasts and audiobooks with their own player
  • Synced lyrics with translation, and song meanings
  • Offline downloads, and playback of your own local files
  • Spotify Connect, Bluetooth, lock screen, widget, Android Auto and Android TV
  • 10-band equalizer, playback speed, data saver and battery saver
  • A private listening recap that stays on your phone
  • 69 languages, AMOLED black and accent colours
  • Community extensions — extra audio sources, lyrics and downloads

What it needs

  • Android 10 or later
  • A Spotify account — free is enough
  • About 58 MB of space for the app

Built with

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, as a native Android app. Two looks ship inside it — Classic and Immersive — switchable in Settings → Display.
